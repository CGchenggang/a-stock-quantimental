## Safety Rules

- Do not delete V1 scripts yet.
- Do not call cached or fallback observations "real-time" without provenance.
- Do not feed legacy single-index regime labels into the validated V2 model as if they were multi-input regimes.
- Do not use legacy proxy fund-flow labels as confirmed capital-flow facts.
- Do not migrate a legacy output until a V2 regression test exists.
