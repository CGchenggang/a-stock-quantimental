## Design Constraints

- Historical decision snapshots are not overwritten by postmortem conclusions.
- Quantitative outputs remain the source of truth; the Agent records evidence and explanations.
- A single realized outcome does not automatically change model weights.
- Outcome data must be based on observations available after the decision time.
